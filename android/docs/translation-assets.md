# Google Translate attribution assets

Downloaded on 2026-10-08 from the badge ZIP linked by Google's
[attribution requirements](https://cloud.google.com/translate/attribution):
https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/translate/images/google-translate-attribution.zip

The app uses the original `png/color-regular@3x.png` and `png/white-regular@3x.png`
without pixel changes. Each is 528×48 pixels and is displayed at its natural
176×16 dp size. The white variant is used on the dark surface, the color variant
on the light surface. Only their resource filenames changed. Google attribution
belongs to translation results and does not imply endorsement of TAGGR.
