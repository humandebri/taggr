## Product Page Copy

### Promotional Text

Join public conversations on TAGGR, follow realms and profiles, share posts and
images, and manage your account with Internet Identity.

### Description

TAGGR is a decentralized social network for public conversations, profiles,
realms, posts, replies, reactions, and user-shared images.

The iOS app is a native SwiftUI client for the existing TAGGR production
service. Browse the public feed without signing in, open posts and profiles,
follow realm discussions, and use Internet Identity to access your account.

Signed-in users can create posts, upload selected images, connect their YouTube
channel to upload selected videos and embed the resulting link, manage profile
settings, read notifications, report posts or users, block users, and use
account wallet surfaces needed for normal TAGGR use.

TAGGR includes public support and privacy routes in the Links section. Moderation
controls are available from post and profile menus after sign-in.

### Keywords

social network,decentralized,community,realms,posts,Internet Identity,ICP

### What's New

Initial iOS release with native feed, profiles, posting, moderation, account
settings, and Internet Identity sign-in.
